## Chapter 5. SPACE IS NOT EMPTY. THE DRIVING MEDIUM

We already know that motion does not arise on its own. It requires contact pressure. This principle works visibly in solids, liquids, and gases. It is logical to expect its continuation at a deeper level — that is, in absolutely all processes of the Universe.

But now a fundamental question arises: if pressure causes motion, how does the pressure itself arise? What is the source of all these actions?
Without an answer to this question, the picture will remain blurred. We will see only effects, never realizing the root cause.

Let’s start with something simple. A person pushes an object. In this case, everything is obvious: the pressure is created by the person, who feels it perfectly. But let us analyze this connection deeper. To press and move an object, a person must engage muscles. Muscle work is the result of complex biochemical processes, which, in turn, are triggered by the rearrangement of molecular bonds.

If we trace the complete chain, it becomes clear: no motion arises "on its own." It is always generated by another motion. One process triggers the next. One form of motion is passed along. And if we do not break this causal chain, the main conclusion becomes inevitable: matter can only be moved by other moving matter.

No "abstract force" comes from the outside. No "energy" starts a process by itself. There is always a specific material object that moves and passes the impulse forward. It is not something abstract acting; it is something concrete moving.

And here we approach the boundary of familiar experience, beyond which lies a domain of absolute ignorance. Moving along the chain, we inevitably run into the question: "What sets the world itself in motion?" Not an individual object, not a mechanism, not a stream of water — but the entire universe at once? What creates pressure at a level where there are no more "hands," "pumps," or "wind"?

"Hands," "pumps," and "wind" are merely the tip of the iceberg, the final elements in a long relay. But what lies at the base of this iceberg?

If space is indeed empty, as modern physics assumes, then there is no answer — and nothing to look for. Too many irreconcilable contradictions are tied to the concept of a vacuum. And the main one is: a vacuum cannot do anything.

It cannot press.    
It cannot transmit motion.    
It cannot serve as a support.

Imagine two objects with an absolute vacuum between them. One of them must somehow affect the other. How will this happen? If there is truly nothing between them, then there is simply no one and nothing to transmit the mechanical action.
One could, of course, use a modern term and say: "a field is acting." This is exactly what official science claims.

But then the next question inevitably arises: what is a field?    
If it genuinely exerts a mechanical effect, it means it must physically exist. It must have a material carrier. It must consist of something and transmit its actions through something. 

Otherwise, we are simply replacing one riddle with another — a physical vacuum with a terminological void.
The statement "the action is transmitted through a field" is not an explanation. It is merely a new name for a hidden mechanism that science cannot describe.

**Why the Concept of a "Physical Field" Is Logically Untenable**

Modern academic science demonstrates high mathematical accuracy in calculations, yet a profound logical contradiction is embedded in its physical foundation. Attempting to explain how one body affects another at a distance through an absolute vacuum, official physics commits a systemic error: it substitutes real mechanical objects with mathematical abstractions.

The chief of these abstractions is the "physical field."
If we strip the complex mathematical apparatus from the equations and turn to the physical nature of the field, we discover that both classical and quantum theories have led physics into a conceptual dead end. The logical untenability of both concepts becomes obvious when analyzing their basic postulates.

**Part 1. The Classical Field: Vibration of "Nothing" in "Nothing"**

In Maxwell’s classical electrodynamics, a field is described as a special continuous "fabric" that fills the space around charges. It does not consist of particles, it has no mechanical boundaries, yet it is supposedly real.
Here, official logic breaks down in three places at once.

**1. The Paradox of the Physical Properties of a Vacuum**

Science claims that a vacuum is an absolute emptiness containing no matter whatsoever. Yet at the same time, this "emptiness" is officially recognized to possess fundamental physical constants: permittivity and permeability. Moreover, the speed of light is calculated directly from them.

But a physical property cannot exist on its own. A property is always a characteristic of something material. To declare that a vacuum has permittivity is like saying that the distance between cities itself has a taste, color, or hardness. Distance, as a characteristic of extension, exists, but it cannot possess its own physical properties until we fill it with a material medium.

**2. The Absurdity of a Wave Without a Medium**

Classical physics acknowledges that light and radio waves are vibrations of the electromagnetic field. But what is a vibration according to the fundamental laws of mechanics? It is a physical process in which the material elements of a medium displace relative to one another.

If the field does not consist of particles, to what exactly does the momentum of motion belong? What specifically compresses, decompresses, and vibrates while a wave travels from the Sun to the Earth? The claim that "space itself" or an "abstract field" vibrates is merely linguistics without a description of the mechanism.

**3. The Infinite Energy of a Point**

If an electron is a structureless material point creating a classical field around itself, then according to Coulomb's law, the energy density of this field at the very center of the particle approaches infinity.

To prevent the electron from simply being ripped apart by its own infinite energy, physicists had to resort to a mathematical trick — the procedure of "renormalization." This is an artificial, purely paper-based subtraction of infinities from infinities, performed solely to rig the formula to fit the required answer.

**The Outcome of the Classical View:**

Scientists and students were forced to believe in the existence of a non-material, almost mystical "aura" (the field) that possesses purely mechanical properties — energy and momentum — yet fundamentally consists of nothing and has no structure. After all, it must act, since there is a real, manifested impact. However, the physical mechanism was replaced by the abstract concept of a "field" and rigged mathematics.

**Part 2. The Quantum Field: Virtual Magic and Mathematical Fraud**

Realizing that vibrations of "nothing in nothing" sounded unconvincing, in the mid-20th century, scientists created Quantum Field Theory (QFT). They declared: fields are numerous — electron, photon, quark fields, and so on. These fields are primary, while the material particles familiar to us are merely "excitations" of these fields, their wave clumps.
To explain how force is transmitted in such a model, QFT introduced the concept of virtual particle exchange. And it is here that the logic of official science completely surrendered to mysticism.

**1. Ghost Particles**

What is a "virtual particle" (for instance, a virtual photon that supposedly transmits magnetic interaction)? The official definition in QFT states: these are particles that fundamentally cannot be detected by any physical instrument. They are born from the vacuum for a fraction of a moment and immediately vanish.

Moreover, these ghosts are officially allowed to travel faster than the speed of light and temporarily violate the law of conservation of energy. Science claims that they borrow energy "on credit" from the emptiness according to the uncertainty principle.

Physics that attempts to explain real mechanical processes through invisible, fundamentally undetectable objects that violate foundational conservation laws is no longer science. It is a religious dogma wrapped in a mathematical package.

**2. The Mechanical Paradox of Attraction**

Imagine two electrons. According to QFT, they exchange virtual photons. The first one fires a photon and receives a backward recoil, while the second catches this photon, absorbs the impact, and flies backward as well. As a result, the electrons repel each other. Here, mechanical logic (albeit with a massive stretch) can still be discerned.

But how does quantum physics explain attraction — for instance, between an electron and a proton? It claims that in this scenario, the virtual photon carries a "negative momentum."
Try to visualize this mechanically: you throw a basketball at a person; the ball flies forward and hits them in the chest, but instead of pushing them away, it pulls them toward you! This is blatant nonsense. Any flying material object can only push and press upon impact.

Quantum physics introduced the concept of "negative momentum" strictly on paper to salvage the minus sign in its equations. To invent "attraction" in a vacuum, they simply flipped the sign in the formula, completely disregarding both physical and common sense.

**3. The Vacuum Catastrophe (The Greatest Miscalculation in History)**

If quantum fields are real, then the vacuum must literally boil with the energy of continuously emerging virtual particles. Physicists attempted to calculate the density of this "vacuum energy."

The result of these theoretical calculations exceeded the real value observed by astronomers by a factor of **10<sup>120</sup>.** That is a one followed by 120 zeros. In any other science, an error in calculations spanning 120 orders of magnitude would lead to the immediate rejection of the theory as false, sending it straight to the dustbin of history. Yet academic physics simply labeled this field calculation an "interesting paradox" and continued to teach students using the exact same textbooks.

To comprehend the staggering scale of this catastrophe, consider the numbers: by official estimates, the entire massive Observable Universe — with all its trillions of galaxies and stars — contains "only" about **10<sup>80</sup>** atoms. This means the total number of atoms in the cosmos is 40 orders of magnitude smaller than the margin of error in the physicists' calculations.

**Summary on Fields**

Over the past century, official physics has fantasized dozens of different, superimposed invisible fields.
The methodological principle of "Occam's Razor" states: entities should not be multiplied beyond necessity. Yet when official science forces dozens of different invisible substances to coexist simultaneously at the exact same point in space, it flagrantly violates this principle. Obviously, such complication in the complete absence of a physical mechanism is a sign of a conceptual dead end.

Imagine an engineer trying to explain how a machine tool works. Instead of describing real gears, belts, and shafts, he declares: "Within the machine, an independent gear field, a belt field, and a shaft rotation field operate simultaneously. They are non-material themselves, but they exchange virtual sheaves with one another."

Any designer would be laughed out of the room for such an approach. But this is precisely what quantum physics did: failing to describe the hidden mechanics of the medium, it simply labeled each of its functions as a separate, mystical field.

**The Only Logical Way Out**

All these logical breakdowns, paradoxes, and mathematical "crutches" are the direct consequence of a single, primal error. Official science stubbornly refuses to acknowledge the obvious: space is not empty.

Emptiness cannot be replaced by non-material, physically impotent fields — whether classical or quantum. There is no mystical "action at a distance." There are no "negative momenta" and no "vibrations of nothingness" with a monstrous error of 120 orders of magnitude.

Nature is designed economically. It has no need for dozens of fictional substances when all observed effects can be explained by the mechanical pressure and vibrations of a single, real medium.

Let us analyze the very fact of a body's motion. If an object is in an absolute vacuum, what does it push against at the moment of acceleration? With what does it resist the beginning of motion? And what forces it to continue its flight when the external push has already stopped?

If there is no continuous material medium between a body and the rest of the world, the entire idea of interaction loses its meaning. It turns out that an object must either move on its own or resist via an "inherent property" within itself, without having any external support.

But this completely contradicts basic physical experience.    
**To press** — you need a support.    
**To transmit pressure** — you need contact.    
**For contact to occur** — a material medium is required.

Emptiness is incapable of providing any of these conditions. But since motion, force, and momentum transfer actually exist, there must be something material between the objects of the physical world through which these processes take place.
Otherwise, the universe turns into a set of unexplainable miracles that a person is asked to simply take on faith.

**Space is not empty. It is filled.** Not with air, not with gas, and not with some bodies familiar to us, but with a deeper, fundamental medium. A medium that is everywhere. A medium in which absolutely all objects of the Universe are immersed — from a microscopic atom to a giant galaxy.

As soon as we restore a fully material, ultra-dense, continuously vibrating medium consisting of real elements back into physics, mathematical abstractions disappear. We no longer need to invent "attraction" through emptiness, we do not need virtual particles with negative momentum, and we do not need to warp a fictional space-time continuum.

Everything falls into its natural causal place:    
**•	There is no attraction** — there is external mechanical pressure from a continuously vibrating medium.    
**•	There is no abstract electromagnetic field** — there is a wave of vibration in a specific material medium.    
**•	There are no virtual ghost particles** — there is direct contact interaction of the elements of the medium with the geometric structure of atoms.

The world is designed simply, logically, and mechanistically. It is time to clear physics of a century of mathematical fantasies.    
In our concept, this medium is called **Aol space.**

It is crucial to rule out a potential misunderstanding right away.    
When we say "medium," one should not imagine something like a fog, gas, or liquid in which physical objects float like fish in water. We are talking about a substance incomparably more dense, fundamental, and universal.

It does not merely "fill the gaps" between bodies. It is precisely what we are used to calling space. Space itself is matter — but matter of a special, baseline level.    

What must this medium be like so that absolutely all physical processes of the universe can occur through it?    
**•	Continuous.** There must be no fractures or empty gaps within it; otherwise, the mechanical transmission of an impact would once again become impossible.    
**•	Capable of colossal pressure.** Without this, it would be unable to set physical objects in motion.    
**•	Capable of high-frequency vibrations.** Otherwise, it is impossible to explain the nature of light, heat, wave radiation, and elementary particles themselves.    
**•	Possess colossal density** to serve as a rigid support for any motion, while remaining all-permeating for macro-objects.

Aol space is exactly this kind of medium.    
Without diving too deeply into the details for now, this medium can be envisioned as a conglomerate of a colossal number of identical primary elements. Later, we will name them aols and dissect their properties in detail.

For now, the core idea itself is what matters to us: a material medium exists between all bodies.     
And this thought fundamentally changes everything.    
**•	It immediately becomes clearer** how the transmission of motion occurs at all. If space is filled, then any mechanical push does not vanish into a vacuum but is translated further through the medium.    
**•	It immediately becomes clearer** how pressure is possible without visible contact between macro-bodies. If there is a material medium between them, then the impact is continuously transmitted along a chain through it.    
**•	It immediately becomes clearer** how light, electricity, magnetism, and gravitational shielding physically exist.

In all these instances, there is no longer any need to invent dozens of different entities. It is enough to acknowledge that the same material mediator is always present between bodies — Aol space.

It is important to emphasize: this medium is not passive. It does not simply "exist."    
Aol space acts dynamically:    
**1.	It presses** with colossal force.    
**2.	It vibrates** at fundamental frequencies.    
**3.	It transmits motion** from element to element.    
**4.	It forms** stable material structures.    
**5.	It destroys** these structures when balance is disrupted.    
**6.	It supports** each and every physical process without exception.

In other words, **Aol space is not the scenery of the cosmos. It is its chief, key participant.**    
And here we approach a thought that will have to be repeated multiple times later, as it is central to our entire concept: the main Mover of all processes in the Universe is Aol space itself. 

•	Not abstract "energy."    
•	Not fictional "fields."

The Mover is precisely the material medium that continuously presses and vibrates. It creates physical objects, holds them in a stable state, reshapes matter, and transfers mechanical impulse.    
In this sense, everything we observe in the physical world — from an elementary particle to a stellar cluster — is merely a different form of behavior of the exact same unified material foundation.    
If one accepts this thought, the entire familiar physical picture of the world begins to rapidly restructure itself.

The baseline phenomena of nature can no longer be explained by old terms:    
**•	Light** is not "photons flying through emptiness."    
**•	Electricity** is not "charges running along wires."    
**•	Gravitation** is not a mystical "attraction of masses" or motion along a warped continuum.

In all these processes, we will now have to ask completely different, purely mechanical questions:

We do not live "alongside" this medium, but inside it. Moreover, we ourselves are part of a world built inside it and out of it. Our bodies consist of the very same elements as space itself, and we vitally require its continuous vibrations and pressure for our very existence.

A paradox occurs: precisely because Aol space is present absolutely everywhere, it ceases to be perceived as a separate object. It begins to seem like a "natural, empty background." And at this moment, man makes a fatal error — he declares this material foundation of the Universe to be an absolute vacuum.
